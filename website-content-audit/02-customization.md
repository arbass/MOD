### FAQ

#### What power densities do you support?
From **5 kW per rack** for standard IT loads up to **150 kW per rack** for GPU-intensive AI workloads.

#### What cooling options are available?
DX cooling for standard densities **(5 to 15 kW/rack)**, chilled water and adiabatic systems for mid-range **(15 to 40 kW/rack)**, and direct-to-chip liquid cooling for high-density GPU configurations (up to 150+ kW).

#### What Tier levels do your data centers support?
We design and build to **Tier I through Tier IV** principles.
